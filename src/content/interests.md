## Groups I admire

The [Oxford Internet Institute](https://www.oii.ox.ac.uk/) (OII) is a multi-disciplinary group that studies the social science of the Internet. This group stands out to me because of the breadth of work they do along with the focus on understanding emerging issues impacting people around the world. Notable projects include [Computational Propaganda](https://demtech.oii.ox.ac.uk/) (now the Programme on Democracy &amp; Technology), [Illegal Wildlife Trade](https://www.oxfordmartin.ox.ac.uk/wildlife-trade/), and [Economic Geographies of the Darknet](https://www.oii.ox.ac.uk/research/projects/economic-geog-darknet/).

[Global Pulse](https://www.unglobalpulse.org/) is a team at the United Nations that focuses on leveraging big data to aid in development and humanitarian action. Their projects span a huge range of topics across the world. Notable projects include [Mobile Data For Sustainable Development & Humanitarian Action](https://web.archive.org/web/20191213133239/https://www.unglobalpulse.org/projects/mobile-data-privacy), [Estimating Migration Flows Using Online Search Data](https://web.archive.org/web/20191211161828/https://www.unglobalpulse.org/projects/migration-search-data), and [Measuring Poverty with Machine Roof Counting](https://web.archive.org/web/20191213131340/https://www.unglobalpulse.org/projects/measuring-poverty-machine-roof-counting) (archived pages — the site has since been restructured).
